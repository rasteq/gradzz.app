### ✦ Description

**Gradzz** ([gradzz.app](https://gradzz.app/)) is an ultra-fast, privacy-first academic GPA & CGPA platform engineered specifically for students of **National University Bangladesh** (জাতীয় বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়).

Covering 4 years of Honours, Degree Pass, and Masters programs across both the 2013–2014 and 2024–2025 syllabuses, Gradzz provides instantaneous multi-year CGPA calculations, print-ready marksheet & official transcript PDF generation, shareable result cards, target grade planning, and OLED dark mode — with zero logins, zero ads, and 100% client-side privacy.
